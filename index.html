Good to know / 03

A few clear answers.

Questions about the collection? Contact BigTree Orders.

How do I access the membership?

Membership enrollment is currently closed. The shop inquiry basket does not create membership or grant access to private material. A ChatGPT subscription does not automatically create an InfoFAQs membership.

Can I pay here?

Online payment is not active. Request a written quote first. No payment details are collected on this page.

Does a purchase include ownership rights?

A physical purchase does not transfer copyright, trademarks, source files or rights to reproduce the artwork. Any separate license must be agreed in writing.

Are private scripts or family images for sale?

No. Private originals, shielded scripts and family images are excluded from this collection.

How is my inquiry handled?

The basket stays in this browser tab and is not submitted automatically. Send inquiry opens your email application with the selected items. Information is sent only when you send that email. Please do not include payment details or private documents.
