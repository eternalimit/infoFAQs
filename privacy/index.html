Privacy information

This page describes the website code currently published in the InfoFAQs repository.

The static pages do not contain an InfoFAQs sign-in system or a form that submits assistant conversations to an InfoFAQs server.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. The homepage requests news through corsproxy.io and may load images from news publishers. Those services receive requests from your browser.

Assistant links open ChatGPT, and training registration opens Google Forms. Information you submit to those services is handled under their own policies and settings.

See GitHub privacy information, OpenAI privacy information, and Google privacy information.

A website link does not establish confidentiality or change a linked service's data settings. Avoid submitting sensitive information unless you understand the receiving service's practices.
