Privacy, plainly.

Saved covers

Your saved cover IDs are stored locally in this browser. No account is required, and this saved list is not sent to an application server by this site. Remove a cover using its Save button or clear your browser data.

Inquiries

The inquiry form prepares an email. Its contents are not submitted to this website. When you send the email, your email provider and the recipient process it.

Site delivery

The website and artwork are served through GitHub Pages. Hosting services may process technical request information. This rebuild adds no third-party analytics, advertising scripts, or payment collection.

Authorization receipts

The separate authorization page retains the browser-local confirmation tool. It does not verify identity or execute actions.
