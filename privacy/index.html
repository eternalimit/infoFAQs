Privacy, plainly.

How these marketplace pages handle your information.

Browsing and saved covers

The new marketplace pages add no advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Saving a cover stores its identifier in this browser’s local storage under bubba.savedCovers. It is not sent to a marketplace server. Use the saved cover button to remove it.

Inquiries

The inquiry form prepares a mailto link; it does not submit messages to this website. Your message goes through your email provider only when you choose to send it. The Copy inquiry button copies the prepared message to your clipboard when permitted.

Hosting and linked services

InfoFAQs is hosted with GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may receive connection information such as your IP address. Linked services, including ChatGPT, operate under their own privacy terms. Existing games and tools may use local browser storage as described on their pages.

Questions

Contact hello@infofaqs.com about this website. Avoid sending sensitive information.
