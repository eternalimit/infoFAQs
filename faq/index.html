BEFORE YOU ASK

A few straight answers.

Can I buy something right now?

The site currently supports browsing and inquiries. Checkout is not open. Ask about a cover to confirm price, format, availability, and delivery before ordering.

Is the cover artwork real?

The displayed images are existing AI-assisted cover artworks from Richard Stein’s collection, rather than website typography standing in for a cover. A cover image does not establish that a full magazine or print edition exists.

Are the prices printed on artwork current?

No. Text inside a cover is part of its artwork. A marketplace price will be stated separately when available.

Where are my saved covers kept?

Only in this browser’s local storage. They are not synced to another device. Clearing browser data removes the saved list.

What happens when I send an inquiry?

The site prepares a message in your email app. You review it and send it yourself. Nothing is submitted to a website inbox by the form.

Can I buy a TCGE sensor here?

No. A functional sensor has not been established as an available product. It is not offered for sale in this collection.
