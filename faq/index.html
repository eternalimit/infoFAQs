Before you ask

A few clear answers.

How the collection, saved covers, and inquiries work.

Can I buy a cover here?

Sales and checkout are not open. Use the inquiry page to ask about a copy, formats, and availability.

Is the displayed design an original cover?

It is a new typographic website preview. It is labeled accordingly; original cover artwork is not displayed.

Does sending an inquiry place an order?

No. This site prepares an email in your email app. You review and send it yourself; no order is created.

Where are my saved covers?

They are stored locally in this browser. They do not sync between devices. Clearing browser storage removes them.

Where are the old tools and games?

Open Tools & Games in the main navigation to reach the existing InfoFAQs pages.

Can I reuse the artwork?

An inquiry or saved cover does not grant reproduction rights. Ask Richard about any intended use.
